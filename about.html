About Dr. Connor Robertson

Dr. Connor Robertson is an entrepreneur, author, and host of The Prospecting Show, based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. His business and publishing work covers sales conversations, prospecting, business systems, and entrepreneurship.

His books include The 7 Minute Phone Call, Built to Run, and Creative Acquisitions. Choose the first for sales conversations, the second for operating systems, and the third for business acquisition questions.

Connor is the founder of Elixir Consulting Group and The Pittsburgh Wire. His title reflects his chiropractic education; this catalog focuses on his business books.
